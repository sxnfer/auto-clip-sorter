# M6 manual test checklist: the redrawn dock and the activity pill (1.1.0)

**What M6 delivers:** the Clip Atlas dock, redrawn to follow your OBS theme ("Native Plus", concept A in
`docs/design/dock-explorations.md`). It arranges itself for where you put it:

- in a **side column**: the game, the Replay Buffer, **Auto-sort** and **Save replay** at the top, then search, game
  chips, a type filter, and your clips grouped by day;
- in the **short bottom row** (next to the Audio Mixer): a rail on the left with the header, and a sideways strip of
  large cards on the right;
- **floating and large**: the list on the left and a preview of the selected clip on the right.

After each save, a note says where the clip was filed, and the new clip is marked **New**. Under the header, an
activity pill shows what Clip Atlas is doing with each save; click it for the details.

**You need:** Windows 10 or 11, OBS Studio 31 or newer, a game you can capture, and the Windows build from the pull
request or the 1.1.0 draft release (see **Download** below).

Write **Pass** or **Fail** in each Result cell. If something fails, open **Help → Log Files → View Current Log**,
search for `clip-atlas`, and paste the nearby lines into Notes. A screenshot helps for anything that looks wrong.

## Download

1. Open the Actions run linked in the pull request (or in my message), or the draft release for 1.1.0. You must be
   signed in to GitHub.
2. On the Actions run, scroll to **Artifacts** and click the one ending in **`windows-x64-<commit>`**. On the draft
   release, download `clip-atlas-1.1.0-windows-x64-installer.exe`.
3. Close OBS and run the installer (as in the M5 checklist). It replaces 1.0.0 and keeps your settings.

## Known limits (expected, not failures)

- With **Auto-sort** off, a saved replay appears in the dock only after **Refresh** (Clip Atlas hears about clips
  when it files them).
- Times and month names follow the Windows regional settings, while the dock's words follow OBS's language.
- The dock reads its green and amber from the theme. A theme that doesn't define them gets Clip Atlas's own.
- `.mkv` files need a codec pack with a thumbnail handler for pictures; without one, the dock shows a tile with the
  clip's type.
